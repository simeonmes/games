How to play

Keyboard

↑ or W gas

↓ or S brake (hold when stopped to reverse)

← or A lean back · → or D lean forward

R restart level · Esc pause · M sound

Menus: arrow keys to move, Enter to pick. No mouse needed.

Touch & gamepad

Touch: ◀ ▶ lean on the left, ▼ brake and ▲ gas on the right.

Gamepad: RT or A gas, LT or B brake, stick or d-pad to lean, Start to pause.

Tips

Land on both wheels. If your helmet touches anything, you crash and go back to the last checkpoint, but the clock keeps running.

Every full flip you land takes 0.5 s off your time.

Beat the star times for up to 3 stars per level. Stars unlock faster bikes.

Saws and spikes are deadly, TNT blows up, and cracked planks fall soon after you touch them.
