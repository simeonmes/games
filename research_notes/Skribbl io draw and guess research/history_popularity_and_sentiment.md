# skribbl.io: History, Popularity, Monetization and Player Sentiment

> Method note: WebSearch worked, but WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (semrush.com, mechanicsofmagic.com, charlatan.ca, greasyfork.org, skribbl.io). Findings below come from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads, so read quotes and numbers as "reported by the snippet". Reddit threads did not show up in search at all, even with r/skribbl phrasing. So the player-sentiment evidence comes from essays (Stanford-style "Critical Play" posts on mechanicsofmagic.com), itch.io and forum comments, Chrome Web Store and Greasy Fork listings, and listicles. Opinion is labeled as opinion.

## 1. Origin and history (who, when, growth moments, redesigns)

### Takeaway
skribbl.io is a free browser Pictionary-style game. It is credited to a solo or small developer, "ticedev", and launched around March 2017. Its biggest growth came during the 2020 lockdowns, when it was used as a Zoom-era social and team-building tool. At some point, probably 2022, it got a major redesign that added mobile support and a reworked toolbar.

### Cited Findings
- Developer is listed as "ticedev". Release is listed as March 2017 (CrazyGames) and as 2017 (KidsSearch). Both are secondary sources; I found no official "about" page. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/skribblio); [KidsSearch](https://www.kidssearch.com/Skribbl.php); also catalogued at [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/skribblio) and [Product Hunt makers page](https://www.producthunt.com/products/skribbl/makers)
- The official site's news section lists "Redesign of the page, Mobile support, Reworked toolbar". The toolbar changes include an undo button and more colors. The site gives no date for these items. — [skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/)
- A third-party guide dates official mobile-browser support to "the game's major 2022 redesign". This is unconfirmed by a primary source. — [mecchachameleon.pro guide](https://mecchachameleon.pro/guides/skribbl-io/)
- A Chrome extension listing mentions a "November 9th, 2022 update" that changed the word list, so the helper no longer contains all possible words. This indirectly supports a significant late-2022 update. — [Chrome Web Store, Skribbl.io Cheats](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/skribblio-cheats/idphhmfinajlkohjcpkcblhddeccokpk)
- Pandemic use: a Canadian student newspaper (The Charlatan) described workplaces using skribbl.io for weekly team games. One health-care start-up had roughly 100 people playing during the week, and the game had "become a way for employees to connect". — [Charlatan](https://charlatan.ca/?p=100073)
- A reviewer called it "probably the online party game that I've played the most over quarantine". — [Study Breaks](https://studybreaks.com/tvfilm/online-party-games)
- It was listed among "online multiplayer games you can play during quarantine". — [Yellow.com.mt](https://www.yellow.com.mt/tips/activity/online-multiplayer-games-you-can-play-during-quarantine/)
- The broader context was a party-game boom on video calls: Jackbox "filled a void brought about by social distancing rules". — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-10-20/jackbox-games-benefits-from-retro-style-party-games-in-pandemic); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/games/the-pandemic-was-transformative-for-jackbox-we-went-from-a-gamer-name-to-a-household-name/)
- One blog says skribbl.io was a niche party tool before 2020 and became a Zoom-replacement social tool in the pandemic. Low reliability: the page has internal date inconsistencies. — [dinogame.gg](https://dinogame.gg/blog/best-browser-games-2020-pandemic/)
- Streamer and community use: players share room links on forums such as vlr.gg ("join this skribbl #5") and itch.io, which shows ad-hoc community lobbies built from private-room links. — [vlr.gg](https://www.vlr.gg/661309/join-this-skribbl-5); [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/11245257)

### Inferences
- The game's growth seems driven by zero-friction access: no account, a link-based private room, and play over voice or video. It did not come from marketing. That fits a solo-dev product.
- The 2022 redesign plus mobile support suggests the developer responded to mobile demand after the pandemic. Mobile is still a pain point (see section 4).
- Unverified background from general knowledge, not sourced here: skribbl.io is widely said to have been inspired by older Flash and Java-era games such as iSketch and Draw My Thing (OMGPOP). iSketch does appear in AlternativeTo's list of alternatives ([AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/isketch-net/)).

### Gaps
- I found no primary interview, press article or official statement about the developer's identity, team size, location or business.
- I found no evidence of an ownership change or acquisition. Absence of evidence is not proof.
- I could not confirm specific Twitch or YouTube streamer moments; searches returned nothing concrete.
- Google Trends data could not be pulled (no fetch access). The expected pattern, a sharp March to May 2020 spike followed by decay, is a hypothesis and not verified.

## 2. Popularity (traffic, lists, classroom and team use)

### Takeaway
skribbl.io is still a large browser game in 2025–2026. Semrush estimates about 7–9 million visits per month with unusually long sessions (around 11 minutes). It is the most consistently named drawing game in "browser games to play with friends" lists.

### Cited Findings
- Semrush reports about 7.16M visits in November 2025, with an average session duration of 11:26. — [Semrush overview](https://www.semrush.com/website/skribbl.io/overview/); [cw.semrush.com mirror](https://cw.semrush.com/website/skribbl.io/overview)
- Semrush's competitor pages list skribbl.io at about 9.3M visits (March 2026 snapshot) and about 9.25M visits (July 2026 snapshot). — [Semrush competitors (nl)](https://nl.semrush.com/website/skribbl.io/competitors)
- Similarweb's comparison page shows skribbl.io with more total visits than codenames.game, and a global rank around #15,791 (snippet; exact visit count not shown). — [Similarweb codenames.game vs skribbl.io](https://www.similarweb.com/website/codenames.game/vs/skribbl.io)
- Semrush and Similarweb track direct competitors such as sketchful.io, drawasaurus.org and scrble.com. — [Semrush sketchful.io](https://www.semrush.com/website/sketchful.io/competitors/); [Semrush drawasaurus](https://www.semrush.com/website/drawasaurus.org/competitors/); [Similarweb scrble.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/scrble.com/competitors/)
- skribbl.io appears in lists of browser games to play with friends. FRVR notes it is easy to set up: open a private room and send the link. — [FRVR](https://frvr.com/blog/guides/best-browser-games-to-play-with-friends/); [PC Gamer best browser games](https://pcgamer.com/best-browser-games); [Windows Report](https://windowsreport.com/browser-games-play-friends/); [YourDictionary](https://wordfinder.yourdictionary.com/blog/10-browser-games-to-play-with-friends-wherever-you-are); [GameSpew Oct 2026](https://www.gamespew.com/2026/10/5-free-multiplayer-browser-games-to-play-with-friends/)
- It is grouped with Gartic Phone and Codenames Online as social browser games with a "Create Private Room" option. — [FRVR](https://frvr.com/blog/guides/lists/best-browser-games-to-play-with-friends/)
- Workplace team-building use (100-person weekly games at a start-up). — [Charlatan](https://charlatan.ca/?p=100073)
- Classroom use is suggested by "skribbl.io unblocked at school" Chrome extensions and by guess-the-picture classroom guides. — [Chrome Web Store, unblocked](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/skribblio-unblocked-at-sc/dkgpifieloncldkhkfbhpcchdanmhpde/support?hl=en); [AhaSlides](https://ahaslides.com/blog/guess-the-picture-game/)

### Inferences
- An 11+ minute average session is long for a browser game. It suggests that once a group forms, people play multiple full games. Retention per session is strong, so the problem is mostly the quality of the experience in public lobbies.
- Visits have stayed roughly flat to slightly growing from 2025 to 2026 (7–9M per month), so the market is mature and not declining.
- The many "unblocked" extensions suggest heavy student play, which matters for content moderation and age-appropriate word lists.

### Gaps
- I could not access Similarweb country or device breakdowns or year-over-year history; fetch was blocked.
- I found no published concurrent-player counts.
- I found no formal educator or teacher writeups specific to skribbl.io; classroom evidence is indirect.

## 3. Monetization (ads, premium, mobile apps)

### Takeaway
skribbl.io is free to play. All evidence points to an ad-supported model with no accounts and no visible premium tier. Several apps on mobile stores use the "Skribbl/Scribble" name, and their official status is unclear.

### Cited Findings
- It is described everywhere as a "Free Multiplayer Drawing & Guessing Game" (the official site title). — [skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/)
- An Apptopia listing shows a Google Play app called "Skribbl.io - Draw, Guess, Have Fun" (package com.Skribbl.io), listed as free. The publisher could not be confirmed as ticedev. — [Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/google-play/app/com.Skribbl.io/about)
- Similar-named mobile apps from other publishers exist: "Scribble IO", "Scribble It! Draw & Guess", "Skriblr" and "Scribble & Guess: IO Game". — [App Store Scribble IO](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scribble-io/id1549656350?uo=4); [App Store Scribble It!](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6670752048); [App Store Skriblr](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skriblr/id6448132675); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.rahuls.scribbleio)
- Competing mobile draw-and-guess apps such as Kwalee's "Draw it" use ad-heavy free models with paid ad removal. — [App Store Draw it](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/draw-it/id1451505313); [Drawize](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1441248659)
- skribbl.io is distributed on portals like CrazyGames, which typically means ad revenue sharing with the portal. — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/skribblio)

### Inferences
- At about 8M visits per month and 11-minute sessions, display and video ads alone could plausibly sustain a solo developer. This is an inference, not a reported figure.
- With no accounts, cosmetics or premium tier, there is room for an original game to add optional cosmetics, private-room perks or a paid "no ads + custom packs" tier without paywalling core play.
- Copycat mobile apps show demand for a native mobile experience that the official game does not clearly provide.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the exact ad formats on skribbl.io (pre-roll, banners), any revenue figures, or whether an official native app exists. The official site could not be fetched.
- I found no player complaints specifically about skribbl.io ads in search results. It is a common anecdotal complaint but not verified here.

## 4. Player complaints

### Takeaway
The documented complaints are mostly about public lobbies: trolls, people who leave or don't draw, cheaters using auto-guess tools, slurs in custom lists, and weak moderation. Scoring is also criticized for underpaying the drawer and rewarding rushed guesses. The word list changes broke helper tools, and the many cheat extensions show that cheating is an ongoing problem.

### Cited Findings
**Trolls, quitters and moderation (opinion and essay)**
- A Critical Play analysis describes players who disconnect or refuse to draw, "which forces the remaining players to" carry the round. It says the "lack of robust moderation in open rooms allows such negative behaviors to go unchecked". It suggests a ranking or skill-matching system. — [mechanicsofmagic.com PDF](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Skribbl-1.pdf)
- Another Critical Play essay describes using vote-kick against cheaters in a public lobby. The author was at first the only one voting, and the chat turned hostile. In another lobby the group readily removed cheaters, which brought players together. The author argues public lobbies lack moderation and suggests a higher minimum age for them. Vote-kick is available "at any time". — [Critical Play: Scribbl.io (2024)](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2024/04/24/critical-play-scribbl-io-2/)
- itch.io comments complain about a racial slur used in a shared skribbl link or room, and one post tracks a "slur counter". This shows custom rooms and words can be weaponized. — [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/11256421); [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/13950428)
- In other games' communities, general complaints about vote-kick systems include abuse, spam and low awareness. These are not skribbl-specific but are relevant design lessons. — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/739630/discussions/0/3200365942100109363); [Don't Starve Together thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/322330/discussions/0/152390014789801338)

**Cheating: auto-guessers, word-list helpers, auto-draw**
- Chrome Web Store has multiple helpers. "Skribbl.io Helper" reads the hint and "finds all possible answers among 3600+ words", sorts by most picked or difficulty, and supports custom lists. "Skribbl Assistant" gives partial-letter matches. "Skribbl Auto Guesser" (beta) auto-sends guesses with a configurable delay and a spam cooldown to avoid kicks. — [Skribbl.io Helper](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/skribblio-helper/pnfncicapicbmndoelcfebipoibdackb); [Skribbl Assistant](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/skribbl-assistant/iglknieoamaemijdclnggoobfpnlljnk); [Skribbl Auto Guesser](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fjnaelnpipplicnabjfdilmncaehfldf); [Skribbl.io Word Assist](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pkehacnkgcbmiogdaoomiiahbeamgoim); [Skribbl.io Bot](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/skribblio-bot/egfjccfngpialgcfdanenhidjhcbbonp/reviews)
- The original Skribbl Assistant (sspathare97) warns it won't work for custom words in private rooms and that clicking hints too fast can get you kicked. — [Greasy Fork Skribbl Assistant](https://greasyfork.org/scripts/407408)
- Greasy Fork hosts several auto-guess scripts, one billed as "the smartest auto-guess tool for skribbl.io", with updates as recent as 2026. — [Greasy Fork scripts for skribbl.io](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/by-site/skribbl.io)
- Spammy "hack" sites advertise auto-draw bots, which "start drawing almost immediately after the prompt is revealed". Low-quality sources, but they show such tools are marketed. — [GamingPirate](https://gamingpirate.com/skribbl-io-hack/); [Wisair](https://www.wisair.com/skribbl-io-hacks/)
- A TASVideos forum thread discusses reading the word from the page's HTML/DOM. Its suggested fix is to render the word and timer into a canvas rather than the DOM. — [TASVideos](https://tasvideos.org/Forum/Topics/20371)
- After the November 9, 2022 update, helper word lists became incomplete, which means the developer changed or expanded the built-in list. — [Chrome Web Store, Skribbl.io Cheats](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/skribblio-cheats/idphhmfinajlkohjcpkcblhddeccokpk)

**Scoring (opinion and essay)**
- Guessers score by speed; the drawer scores per correct guesser. — [progameguides](https://progameguides.com/skribbl/what-is-skribbl-io-how-to-play-it)
- Critical Play analyses found that the drawer earned fewer points than the first guesser, and in one analysis less than even the last guesser. This "does not provide an incentive for the drawer to draw well". Players also learned to guess quickly even when unsure, because being first pays most. — [Critical Play: Bluffing, Judging, and Getting Vulnerable (2022)](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2022/04/15/critical-play-bluffing-judging-and-getting-vulnerable-skribbl-io-tristan-wang/); [Critical Play: Judging – Skribbl.io](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/?p=27382); [Critical Play: Judging and Getting Vulnerable (2024)](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2024/05/07/critical-play-judging-and-getting-vulnerable-29/)

**Word lists**
- Many third-party custom word-list pages exist, including "dirty" lists. This shows demand for themed and adult packs and a risk of offensive content in custom rooms. — [UMB-hosted spam page "skribblio custom words list dirty"](https://siegfriedt-survey.umb.edu/pti/skribblio-custom-words-list-dirty) (low-quality source; cited only as evidence that the demand exists)

### Inferences
- Exposing the word in client state, plus predictable hint patterns and a known 3,600-word list, makes helper-based cheating trivial. An original game should validate guesses on the server only, never send the word to guessers' clients, and use larger, rotating, or player-generated word pools.
- The scoring structure (first guess pays most, drawer underpaid) creates "spam guess fast" behavior and low drawer effort. Both are documented in the essays.
- Moderation tools are limited to vote-kick. Racial slurs in room links and custom words point to a need for profanity filtering on custom words and names, report tools, and safer public defaults for a young audience.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads surfaced. These complaints were not verified there: writing the word on the canvas, obscure word lists, lag or disconnects, ads, the small mobile canvas, and language issues. They are commonly cited in community lore, but I found no source in this session.
- I found no developer response to cheating or scoring criticism.

## 5. Wishlist and feature requests (including what clones and extensions add)

### Takeaway
Extension and clone behavior shows what players want: word hints, solver tools, custom lists, more players per room, better drawing tools (fill and undo were added late), mobile support, and different game modes like Gartic Phone's telephone format. I found no official wishlist.

### Cited Findings
- The official redesign added undo, more colors, a reworked toolbar and mobile support, which suggests these were requested gaps before then. — [skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io/)
- The community wiki lists a fill tool (hotkey F) and undo (hotkey U). Guides describe the toolset as brush, fill bucket and eraser. I found no evidence of a line or shape tool, layers, or replay. — [skribbl-io fandom wiki: Drawing](https://skribbl-io.fandom.com/wiki/Drawing); [iogameguide](https://iogameguide.com/guides/skribbl-io-guide)
- Extensions add word suggestions, sorting by difficulty or popularity, custom word lists, auto-guessing, and cooldown controls. — [Skribbl.io Helper](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/skribblio-helper/pnfncicapicbmndoelcfebipoibdackb); [Skribbl Auto Guesser](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fjnaelnpipplicnabjfdilmncaehfldf)
- Greasy Fork has many userscripts for the site, including the auto-guessers above (fetch blocked, so the full feature inventory was not verified). — [Greasy Fork by-site](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/by-site/skribbl.io)
- Clones: Drawasaurus and Sketchful "copy the loop almost exactly" (80-second rounds, scaling points). Skribbl, Drawasaurus, Sketchful and Drawize all support custom word lists in private rooms. Gartic Phone changes the loop: "no live guessing, no time pressure". — [dinogame.gg games like skribbl](https://dinogame.gg/blog/games-like-skribbl-io/) (moderate reliability)
- An AlternativeTo user review says Sketchful is "extremely similar to skribbl.io" but allows more players. — [AlternativeTo Sketchful](https://alternativeto.net/software/sketchful-io)
- AlternativeTo lists many alternatives (gartic.io, Drawasaurus, Pictar.io, Doodle Duel, Krabbl.io, Drawaria.online, LetsDraw.It, iSketch, SketchAI), which shows a crowded clone market. — [AlternativeTo skribbl alternatives](https://alternativeto.net/software/skribbl/)
- Design suggestions from the Critical Play essays: skill-based matchmaking or ranking; guessers rating the drawer to award drawer points; paying a drawer who gets everyone to guess as much as the fastest guesser; zero drawer points if nobody guesses; higher minimum age for public lobbies. — [Skribbl-1 PDF](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Skribbl-1.pdf); [Critical Play 2024](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2024/04/24/critical-play-scribbl-io-2/); [Critical Play: Judging](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/?p=27382)

### Inferences
- Features to consider for an original game, based on documented gaps (my synthesis):
  - Line and shape tools, brush opacity and stabilization (no shape tools are evident in skribbl).
  - Replay and timelapse or GIF export of each drawing, and an end-of-game gallery. Gartic Phone's appeal suggests people love reviewing the drawings afterward.
  - Curated themed word packs with difficulty tiers.
  - Anti-cheat built in from the start.
  - A mobile-first canvas.
  - Drawer-friendly scoring.
- These features were not confirmed as player requests in this session: dark mode or custom themes, layers, replay/GIF, gallery saving, line or shape tools. They are plausible but unsourced.

### Gaps
- I could not access Reddit or Product Hunt comments to gather direct player requests.
- I could not fetch the full Greasy Fork inventory, which might list theme, gallery or drawing-tool scripts.

## 6. What makes a good round fun

### Takeaway
Design writing and reviews agree that the fun comes from time pressure, laughing at imperfect drawings, a low skill barrier, and good-faith cooperation between drawer and guessers. Rules that discourage guessing or penalize failure tend to kill the social fun.

### Cited Findings
- The appeal is "laughing at your friends' attempts to draw things under pressure". — [Tabletop Gaming via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/tabletop-gaming/20210625/282376927542830)
- "No great drawing talent is required": success depends on imagination, empathy and communicating under restrictions (Pictomania review). — [Bumbling Through Dungeons](https://bumblingthroughdungeons.com/pictomania-board-game-review/)
- Time limits are a key lever that "directly influences the gameplay dynamics" by pushing speed. — [Draughts London games](https://games.draughtslondon.com/?p=3166) (via search snippet)
- Penalties for wrong guesses would discourage the good-faith guessing and drawing that drives social fun. "The interplay between these two rules serves to create the game's main types of fun." — [mechanicsofmagic.com essay](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/?p=26690)
- Easy, medium, hard and funny word tiers keep rounds accessible to beginners and challenging for experienced players. — [Legit.ng Pictionary ideas](https://www.legit.ng/ask-legit/1648036-pictionary-ideas-easy-medium-hard-ideas-category/); [AhaSlides](https://ahaslides.com/blog/guess-the-picture-game/)
- Critical Play essays describe skribbl.io's fun as "bluffing, judging and getting vulnerable": drawing badly in public is a vulnerable, bonding act. — [Critical Play: Bluffing, Judging, and Getting Vulnerable](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2022/04/15/critical-play-bluffing-judging-and-getting-vulnerable-skribbl-io-tristan-wang/)

### Inferences
- A good round needs:
  - A word that is guessable in time but not trivial.
  - A drawer motivated to communicate, which means scoring has to reward the drawer.
  - Visible progress for guessers: hints, near-miss "close!" feedback, live reactions.
  - A shared payoff moment, such as reveal and replay.
- Near-miss feedback and a post-round replay or gallery fit the "laugh at the drawings" core loop.

### Gaps
- I found no formal published postmortem from the skribbl.io developer, and no academic study of draw-and-guess fun beyond student essays.
