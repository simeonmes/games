SKYWARD

One climb. No checkpoints. How high can you go?

A fan-made game inspired by Only Up! — an original world, built in code.

How to play

MoveW A S D / arrows · left stick · joystick (left side)
LookMouse (click the game first) · right stick · drag on the right side
JumpSpace · A / ✕ · Jump button
SprintShift · bumpers / triggers · push the joystick all the way, or Sprint
ZoomMouse wheel
PauseEsc · Start
  • Jump at a ledge that's a bit too high and you'll grab it and pull yourself up.
  • Mushrooms bounce you high. Fans blow you up into the sky. Grab zip lines by jumping into them.
  • Ice is slippery, cardboard and cracked ice crumble, conveyor belts drag you, and wind gusts push you.
  • In space, gravity is weaker: you'll jump much further.
  • There are 12 rubber ducks hidden just off the path.
  • Too hard? Turn on Easy mode in Settings for checkpoints.
