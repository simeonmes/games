SQUINT

Draw it. Guess it. Before the clock runs out.

About

Squint is a free online drawing and guessing game for 2 to 20 friends.

A game has a few rounds. Every round, each player takes a turn drawing a secret word while everyone else races to guess it. Guess fast to score more points. The player with the most points at the end wins!

How to play

  1. Create a private room and send your friends the invite link or the 6-digit code.
  2. On your turn, pick one of the words and draw it.
  3. Everyone else types guesses in the chat. Letters are revealed as hints.
  4. The faster you guess, the more points you get. The drawer scores when people guess.
  5. After the last round, the top three take the podium.

Rules

  • No writing the word or its letters on the canvas.
  • Don't give away the answer in chat once you've guessed it.
  • Be nice! You can votekick players who spoil the fun.

Drawing keys: B brush · F fill · U undo · C clear · S swap colours · 1–5 size. Right-click draws with your second colour.
