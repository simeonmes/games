MOUNT VEIL Chapter 1 · The Foothills
How to play

Keyboard

←→↑↓ or WASD move

Esc pause

Gamepad & touch

Stick or d-pad moves · A jump · X/B dash · bumpers or triggers grab · Start pause

On a touchscreen: stick on the left, Jump / Dash / Grab on the right.

Moves

Dash in any of 8 directions. Your hair turns blue when it's used; landing or a green crystal gives it back.

Grab walls to climb. Climbing tires you out (you'll flash red), so rest on the ground.

Jump off walls. Strawberries follow you and count once you land somewhere safe.
