CELESTE A climb up Mount Veil
How to play

Keyboard

←→↑↓ or WASD move

Esc pause

Gamepad & touch

Stick or d-pad moves · A jump · X/B dash · bumpers or triggers grab · Start pause

On a touchscreen: stick on the left, Jump / Dash / Grab on the right.

Moves

Dash in any of 8 directions. Your hair turns blue when it's used; landing or a green crystal gives it back.

Grab walls to climb. Climbing tires you out (you'll flash red), so rest on the ground.

Jump off walls. Strawberries follow you and count once you land somewhere safe.

The mountain, and the people on it, will teach you the rest. In a scene, press jump, dash or Enter to continue.

Music

Play your own songs while you climb. Pick audio files from your device (MP3, M4A, OGG, WAV…). They stay in this browser and are never uploaded. With several songs, the music changes as you climb higher, in file-name order.

    Save file

    Progress is saved in this browser. Clearing site data erases it, so download a copy to keep it safe or move it to another device.

    Places you've been

    Climb a finished chapter again, for strawberries or a better time. A golden strawberry waits at the start: carry it to the end without dying. Find a chapter's hidden cassette to open its B-Side. Your story carries on from where it is.
